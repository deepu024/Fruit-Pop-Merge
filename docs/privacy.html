Privacy Policy

Fruit Pop Merge · Last updated: 8 November 2026

Fruit Pop Merge (“the app”) is a free, ad-supported puzzle game published by Deepanshu Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information ourselves. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app chat. Your game progress — scores, settings and discovered fruits — is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language),
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data.

This data is transmitted encrypted to Google. How Google uses it is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before personalized ads are shown we ask for your consent using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the app.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional — you choose whether to watch one.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game data. For data processed by Google, see Google's policies above.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
